# NL-BIOMERO 1.8.0-beta.16: fixes from ACC acceptance of beta.15

Status on 20 November 2026: **in preparation**. ACC is still testing
[1.8.0-beta.15](https://github.com/NL-BioImaging/NL-BIOMERO/releases/tag/v1.8.0-beta.15);
its final report is due on 23 November 2026. This beta carries only the fixes
for findings that block acceptance. Stable 1.8.0 is released from the beta ACC
accepts.

## ACC findings on 1.8.0-beta.15

ACC reports findings as commits on its deploy branch; the coordinator files
them as issues and answers here. Board: [Release = 1.8.0-beta.16](https://github.com/orgs/NL-BioImaging/projects/2?filterQuery=release%3A%221.8.0-beta.16%22).

| Finding | Issue | Decision | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concurrent uploads of one generated job script share a staging file, so a fresh batch can fail (`size mismatch in put!`) | [#119](https://github.com/NL-BioImaging/NL-BIOMERO/issues/119) | Blocks acceptance: regression in core 2.9.0b29 | [biomero#124](https://github.com/NL-BioImaging/biomero/issues/124), fixed by [biomero#125](https://github.com/NL-BioImaging/biomero/pull/125) (merged) |
| A workflow stays failed after Slurm requeues and completes a node-failed job | [#120](https://github.com/NL-BioImaging/NL-BIOMERO/issues/120) | Later: NODE_FAIL was already final in core 2.9.0b25, not a regression | - |
